
# About Me

Hello world, this is Ninenite. I speak both Cantonese and English.

I've been playing osu! tournament since 2019 and have been part of the osu! community tournament staff since 2021. You can check out my [osu! profile](https://osu.ppy.sh/users/9956692).

Also a Visual Novel enjoyer, with interest in photography, listening to music, drinking tea, hobby coding side projects, and audiophile gear. As well as a beginner in electric guitar.

Why I started this blog... It seems that Instagram can't fit all my projects and present it in a tidy way, meanwhile inspired by my favorite blogger [KirimaSharo](https://kirimasharo.com).
